What is X6?

X6 Spectral Astrolabium is an open research workshop maintained by Thomas Gfeller. It brings together small browser labs, reproducible mathematical examples and research plans. The shared question is: what can we reliably learn from measured signals and relationships between events?

Think of a toolbox accompanied by a lab notebook. A tool calculates something; the notebook explains its inputs, results and limits. A successful example does not make every tool suitable for every purpose.

What can you use today?

  • Browser applications for signals and finite causal orders.
  • Computational references with source code, test cases and documented counterexamples.
  • A local workflow for running, verifying and exporting experiments.
  • Research plans for further mathematics, industrial applications and laboratory work.

Newly documented: the first proper-time reference is extended with explicit assumptions, finite-sample uncertainty and sequential sampling. The “Causal orders” chapter explains results, budget limits and the bounded chain diagnostic.

X6 is a public research preview. It does not establish a general spacetime theory, a qualified quantum chip or superior industrial denoising. This describes the available evidence, rather than deciding which questions might eventually be solved.

New: proper time v0.2 explains when additional data help, when density errors invalidate intervals and when a conservative analysis refuses precision.

New: 16,384 fresh clouds test a restricted de Sitter curvature estimator. Extra chains add little precision; “Causal orders” explains the result and the next research step.

New: two-element chains now yield conservative curvature intervals with mathematically justified coverage in the known uniform model. Fresh tests also show that the intervals remain too wide for 20% precision.

New: the sharper KL bound improves sufficient data budgets 2.71–2.77 fold. 77,824 fresh clouds show initial 20% precision qualifications within the known de Sitter family; intervals remain too wide at smaller curvatures.

New: an analytical variance certificate improves sufficient curvature budgets 2.89–4.10 fold against KL. 45,056 fresh clouds, a separate geometry audit and a review packet document both the advance and its limits.
