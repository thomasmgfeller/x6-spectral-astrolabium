What do the project names mean?

NamePlain-language purposeImportant limit
Lorentz / causal ordersStudies relationships, structural features and geometric estimation tasks.Matching signatures do not automatically mean identical orders.
RAX / RAX-CQGA relational research programme and architecture for further questions about quantum dynamics.The programme and finite benchmarks are not an established physical theory.
X6-QEExact graph examples about spectra and lost structural information.Not a general qualification of quantum or spacetime claims.
Industrial denoisingInvestigates useful signal reconstruction for machinery.Limited data pilot; no demonstrated superiority of a new filter.
ACVM / Sagnac / photonicsControl channels, reference workflows and optical experiment designs.Software checks and firmware builds do not replace electrical or optical measurement qualification.
Edge ReferenceLocal execution and export as a starting point for applications near devices.Not a generally qualified distributed hardware product.
NQK, Experiment Fabric, Neutral DiscoverySeparate research sources or dossiers listed in the module register.Some are only on research branches; historical NQK test archives are missing.

Status: reproducible laboratory machine reference completed. Clarified on 17 October 2026, this label covers the reproducible secondary analysis of 20 specified Paderborn laboratory bearings using three classical reference methods. The envelope reference detected four of four damaged evaluation bearings and raised a false alarm for one of two healthy bearings. These small counts establish neither added value from a new filter nor industrial performance. Industrial confirmation remains NOT_READY. The measurement data retain their non-commercial CC BY-NC 4.0 licence; external person-level replication and physical device qualification remain open.
