## Lorentzian spectral geometry

The complete exact test of 2,045 seven-element causal orders refutes the two-moment H7 hypothesis: two nonisomorphic pairs remain. Inspect all counterexamples and reproduce the separate enumeration and algebra checks. This finite result does not establish a physical theory.

## Read the scope with the result

This is experimental research software. A passing software test is not physical validation. Hardware qualification, independent external replication and industrial performance qualification remain separate open tasks.

- [BK phases 1/2: implemented; curvature remains on hold](https://thomasmgfeller.github.io/x6-spectral-astrolabium/modules/lorentz-local-dalembert.html): 48 independent Minkowski test clouds fully replayed. Distance rankings improve, but neighborhood coverage and metric accuracy fail acceptance. Code, protocol and the HOLD result are published.
- [BD versus C2: comparison complete](https://thomasmgfeller.github.io/x6-spectral-astrolabium/modules/lorentz-bd-reference.html): 6,528 Poisson clouds fully replayed. The BD pilot fails the prespecified utility gate; variable curvature stops within this budget. Data, limitations and a separate local-operator pilot are available.
- [Cloud variance: another verified budget gain](https://thomasmgfeller.github.io/x6-spectral-astrolabium/modules/lorentz-de-sitter-variance.html): Analytical variance improves sufficient budgets 2.89–4.10 fold against KL. 45,056 fresh clouds and a separate geometry audit; a counterexample remains visible.
- [Sharper intervals: lower data budgets](https://thomasmgfeller.github.io/x6-spectral-astrolabium/modules/lorentz-de-sitter-kl.html): Same finite-sample 95% coverage in the known model; sufficient budgets improve 2.71–2.77 fold. 77,824 fresh clouds show initial 20% precision qualifications.
- [Curvature intervals: controlled, still imprecise](https://thomasmgfeller.github.io/x6-spectral-astrolabium/modules/lorentz-de-sitter-c2.html): Enclosed geometry and at least 95% coverage in the known uniform model. 16,384 fresh clouds: no 20% precision qualification.
- [Curvature: how much information do chains add?](https://thomasmgfeller.github.io/x6-spectral-astrolabium/modules/lorentz-de-sitter-information.html): 16,384 fresh clouds: extra chains reduce estimation error by only 1.37%. The 10% target fails; data budgets and limitations are documented.
- [Proper time v0.2: where inference remains valid](https://thomasmgfeller.github.io/x6-spectral-astrolabium/modules/lorentz-proper-time-validity.html): 1,176 deterministic combinations: density errors, missing observations, conservative intervals and explicit point budgets.
- [Curvature: results and limits](https://thomasmgfeller.github.io/x6-spectral-astrolabium/modules/lorentz-curvature.html): 8,702 orders: sensitivity target missed. Density twins show why an alarm cannot uniquely identify curvature.
- [RSS: fixed point count](https://thomasmgfeller.github.io/x6-spectral-astrolabium/modules/lorentz-rss-fixed-n.html): Conditional chain moments, 24,576 clouds and explicit precision limits.
- [Signals & validation](https://thomasmgfeller.github.io/x6-spectral-astrolabium/modules/signal-lab.html): Synthetic signals, reference methods, replayable evidence.
- [Spectral graph references](https://thomasmgfeller.github.io/x6-spectral-astrolabium/modules/x6-qe.html): Exact finite examples and limits of spectral distinguishability.
- [RAX · relational research](https://thomasmgfeller.github.io/x6-spectral-astrolabium/modules/rax-programme.html): Predictive reductions, competing models and falsification.
- [Photonics & control](https://thomasmgfeller.github.io/x6-spectral-astrolabium/modules/acvm-optical.html): Software references and planned physical acceptance tests.
- [Industrial data pilot](https://thomasmgfeller.github.io/x6-spectral-astrolabium/modules/industrial-pilot.html): Status: reproducible laboratory machine reference completed. 20 specified laboratory bearings; industrial confirmation remains open.
- [NQK research dossier](https://thomasmgfeller.github.io/x6-spectral-astrolabium/modules/nqk.html): A separate research direction with incomplete reproduction archives.
- [Proper time and uncertainty](https://thomasmgfeller.github.io/x6-spectral-astrolabium/modules/lorentz-proper-time.html): Known flat model: inspect assumptions, uncertainty bounds, sequential sampling and chain warnings.
