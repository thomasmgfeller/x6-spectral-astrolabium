Changes made for this pilot: selected one metadata-qualified repeat per
physical bearing and the vibration_1 channel; retained the first 256000
stored samples as unchanged float64 values; omitted an extra endpoint
where present and omitted other channels; lossless NPZ compression.
No filtering, interpolation, resampling or amplitude conversion of these
redistributed input values. Exact archive/member/signal hashes and selection
attempts are in manifest.json. No endorsement by the original authors is implied.
