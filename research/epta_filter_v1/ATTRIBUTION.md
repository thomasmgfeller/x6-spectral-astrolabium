# EPTA DR2 timing data

J. Antoniadis et al. (EPTA Collaboration), *The second data release from the European Pulsar Timing Array I. The dataset and timing analysis*, version 2.1. Zenodo. [Version DOI and author list](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8300645) · [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202346841).

The published data carry [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Retain this credit and identify changes when sharing. X6 selected eight DR2full sources, computed fixed-model residuals with TEMPO2, grouped observing times, simulated signals and calculated the filter comparison. The CSV is an X6 processing of the EPTA measurements under CC BY 4.0. EPTA supplied the observations and timing models. X6 is responsible for this comparison.

[TEMPO2](https://bitbucket.org/psrsoft/tempo2/) runs as a separately installed executable. [Hobbs, Edwards & Manchester (2006)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2006.10374.x) · [Edwards, Hobbs & Manchester (2006)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2006.10870.x).
