# Transport of fixed-anchor sub-observation tests

The first campaign uses a deliberately bounded set of moment and Hellinger
inequalities. A separate frozen follow-up reuses probability certificates
already published for single-support motions. This is a conditional-law
argument, independent of how much the joint operator changes.

For one labelled interval with moving endpoint i and fixed endpoint k in
{0,1,2,3,4}, its Poisson marginal depends only on i, k and the fixed density.
Moving any other support leaves this marginal unchanged. Hence the exact
single-count probability event from a source LAW_EXCLUDED cell lower-bounds
the TV of the entire paired experiment whenever that support's displacement
lies in the certified source interval. No product of overlapping counts is
formed. For the inherited joint event on I(2,15), I(15,1), the same argument
uses their joint law: the intervals are disjoint in time and only node 15
moves among their endpoints. Their independent Poisson increments remain
valid when any other support moves.

For source sign sigma and source step s>=0, a paired line with velocity v_i
has identical endpoint position precisely when s=v_i*h/sigma. All mapping
and interval intersections are exact rational operations, including v_i<0.
Intersect the pullbacks of the source LAW_EXCLUDED regions and the one joint
region with each original HOLD cell, retaining all endpoints. Every resulting
subcell either cites a source region containing its entire displacement range
or remains HOLD. Adjacent closed source regions cover their shared boundary.

The source means and probability certificates are pinned to unchanged evidence;
the audit verifies that all event endpoints other than the selected moving
support are fixed, and independently checks geometry/mean invariance. It is
not valid to transfer a single-support gap certificate, because the second
moving support also changes the operator; such certificates are excluded from
this procedure. Likewise, selected support-support order certificates are not
transported. Law exclusions apply only to the unchanged-order subset, where
the observation intervals remain in the fixed outer diamond.

This stage changes neither the initial frozen budget nor its HOLD archive. Its
rule and source evidence are frozen before the transfer is evaluated. It adds
no new search directions, probability threshold tuning or unaccounted mass.
