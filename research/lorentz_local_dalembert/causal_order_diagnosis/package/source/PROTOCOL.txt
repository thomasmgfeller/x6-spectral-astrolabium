FOUR EXTERNAL LANDMARKS: INTRINSIC SELECTION AND NEW-CLOUD PILOT
Frozen before generation, 2026-10-21. Local prospective protocol, not a public
preregistration. All historical HOLD gates remain regardless of this pilot.

MODEL AND SEPARATION
32 independent selection clouds A at rho_A=32768 in a flat 2+1 diamond of
duration 1.5. The inherited generator samples the inner duration-1 diamond
and independent outer shells; its complete source snapshot is vendored.
Root, support and the first three landmarks are selected by the existing
order/count-only joint procedure with its unchanged independent-recolouring
diagnostic. The recolouring is not claimed to create independent physical data.

A fourth external landmark is selected using A only, as specified in contract.
The selector sees the complete order, marks, interval counts and declared
density. It does not receive coordinates, fields or B counts. The first three
landmarks and support identities are not reselected if the fourth fails.
Known labelled boundaries and inner/outer sampling domains are inherited
experimental metadata; this is not reconstruction from a bare unlabeled order
with unknown density. The search is bounded, not a nonexistence proof.

After sealing selection, nine independently generated Poisson count clouds B_r
at rho_B=32768 in the same outer diamond measure all required intervals. This
is equivalent in law to independently labelled Poisson components at a higher
total intensity; it does not reclassify a fixed set of points independently.
Conditional on A, the chosen endpoint locations and the counting regions are
fixed, so their B counts do not carry A's selection-on-count bias. The finite
nonlinear transformation N^(2/3) remains biased. No bias correction is fitted.
Each interval between A endpoints is contained in the outer causal diamond.

Primary B_0 is fixed in advance. B_1,...,B_8 measure conditional repeated-count
variability only. They do not change the primary weights or selection. Their
mean is not substituted for B_0. Each run stores seeds, counts, overlaps,
source/coordinate/interval hashes, complete failures and inference seals.

FOUR-LANDMARK OPERATOR
q_ab = [N_B(I(a,b))/(rho_B*pi/12)]^(2/3), for timelike pairs.
psi_i(x) = [q_ai,x - q_a0,x - q_ai,o + q_a0,o]/2.
For exact distances psi_i(x)=<a_i-a_0,x-o>_eta.
G_ik=[q_ai,ak - q_ai,a0 - q_a0,ak]/2.
With scale s fixed on A, use z=psi/s^2, h=G/s^2. Solve the full KKT system
for min a^T(I+11^T)a subject to D(z)a=t(h), where linear targets vanish and
quadratic targets are twice the entries of h. Full physical weights are
(-sum(a),a)/s^2. The shared field value at the root is included exactly.

The independent-B three-landmark baseline uses the original first three
landmarks plus root-to-support counts from the SAME B realization and the
SAME root/support/scale selected on A. This makes the main comparison paired
at the same sampled-point budget. The old three-landmark operator measured
on A and the true-coordinate Oracle are diagnostics only.

COUNT CORRELATIONS
For fixed intervals I_e,I_f conditional on A:
  Cov(N_e,N_f | A) = rho_B Vol(I_e intersection I_f).
The intersection count K_ef is an unbiased conditional estimator of that
quantity. In particular K_ee=N_e. K is a positive-semidefinite incidence Gram
matrix. This identity follows by decomposing the two regions into shared and
disjoint Poisson components. It requires independent B; treating A-selected
counts as though their endpoints were fixed would not justify the same claim.

At the observed counts, C_q = J_q K J_q^T with
  (J_q)_ee = (2/3) q_e/N_e
and the additional s^-2 when q is normalized. This is the first-order delta
approximation, not an exact covariance for transformed counts.

The full KKT sensitivity is evaluated analytically. With
  M=[H_noise D^T; D 0], M u=[0;t], u=(a,lambda),
  M du=[-dD^T lambda; dt-dD a].
The derivative includes all shared landmark intervals, root terms, mixed
monomials and the metric target. Propagate C_weights=J_weights C_q J_weights^T.
Its rows sum to zero because the full operator annihilates constants.
No independent-coordinate or independent-interval assumption is introduced.

Negative control: diagonalize K before the identical propagation. Both full
and diagonal predictions are compared against the same held-out B repeats.
The full covariance is used for uncertainty prediction; this protocol does
not claim a new covariance-optimized choice of the operator weights.

GEOMETRY AND FIELD NOISE ARE SEPARATE
For each fixed A and analytic evaluation field f, predicted repeated-count
variance is f^T C_weights f, compared with the sample variance of responses
on B_1,...,B_8. All nine weight solutions must be available to enter that
calibration summary; the number of excluded A clouds is reported. This
complete-case restriction can bias the variance comparison and is a reason
for the minimum-availability gate. It is not a confidence interval for the
continuum d'Alembertian. Systematic geometry/discretization bias remains.

IID field-noise cost at sigma_f=0.001 is separately sigma_f^2 ||w||^2.
This is an analytic diagnostic, not a newly observed sensor measurement.
The count covariance is not added to a previously estimated total residual
covariance. Counting noise and field noise are independent by construction.

QUALIFICATION AND COMPARISON
Report every case. A four-landmark solution is qualified only if the Lorentz
signature, rank, condition and KKT checks pass AND its reconstructed order
on all landmarks/root/supports exactly equals the observed order. There is
no signature clipping, coordinate-truth alignment, ordering projection or
replacement of failures. Raw valid weights which fail order are retained as
diagnostics and never labelled qualified measurements.

Primary accuracy statistics compare all paired B_0 weight-valid cases;
qualified-four subsets are separately reported. Their availability must
also pass. Cloud bootstrap samples whole A clouds with all four fields
together. Neither fields nor B repeats increase the count of independent A
clouds. Bootstrap intervals are descriptive pilot intervals, not simultaneous
coverage guarantees for all gates. Thresholds and seeds are in contract.json.
All gates are needed for PILOT_SUPPORTED; otherwise HOLD.

COMPUTATION AND AUDIT
Two bounded worker processes, 16 assigned A seeds each. Every scheduled seed
is accounted for; no replacements. Pure synthetic preflight tests verify exact
distance recovery, KKT derivatives via differences, relabelled selection,
Poisson overlap covariance and the independent-count negative control.
Sage verifies exact geometric identities and covariance algebra. Subsequent
replay checks use the sealed records, not outcome-adjusted rules.
