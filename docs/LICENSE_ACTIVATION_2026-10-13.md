# Apache-2.0 activation — 13 October 2026

The maintainer explicitly authorized the general Apache-2.0 release of
Astrolabium and NQK on 13 October 2026 (Europe/Zurich). The complete official
license is now provided in `LICENSE`, with attribution in `NOTICE` and the
distribution boundary in `LICENSE_SCOPE.md`.

This supersedes the earlier decision to defer the project license. It does
not record a new warranty about third-party ownership or an independent
clearance of all AI-assisted or historical material. Existing MIT and other
separate licenses and third-party rights remain intact.

Both repository branches (`main`, `research/nqk`) receive the licensing update.
NQK remains a separate research programme. The separately hosted NQK Lab also
receives these license files through its own source and publication workflow.
No public-access setting is changed by this licensing decision.

This change updates license/status documents, citation license metadata and
the public landing page. Astrolabium application code, agent schedules and
pinned research sources are unchanged. No old PR is merged. The historical
`SHA256_REPOSITORY.json` and scientific evidence are retained; the accompanying
`LICENSE_ACTIVATION_MANIFEST.json` hashes the files in this licensing change.
No new numerical or physical validation is claimed.
